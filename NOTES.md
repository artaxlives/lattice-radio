## Changed from the plan
None in scope or architecture. The single HTML file includes original signal-themed prose, a word-level model for orders 2–4, weighted sampling, temperature and seed controls, reset, a live candidate panel, and gesture-enabled oscillator synthesis. Corpus-frequency smoothing keeps at least seven real candidate probabilities visible even for sparse contexts. An initial 42-word generated burst makes the broadcast immediately demonstrable, followed by one word every 180 ms. The signal bars reflect word steps; they are not an audio waveform.

## Could not do
None. Verification uses a local Node DOM harness, including initial rendering, candidate counts, order and seed changes, reset, and mocked Web Audio scheduling. No build step or dependencies are required.

## Omitted for lack of a service
None. The application runs entirely locally without accounts, APIs, fonts, images, or runtime network requests.
